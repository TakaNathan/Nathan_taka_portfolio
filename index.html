Ottawa, Canada

I'm Nathan Taka Tsaga, a first-year Software Engineering student in the co-operative program at the University of Ottawa. Originally from Douala, Cameroon, I made the leap to Canada driven by one goal: becoming an engineer who builds technology that actually matters.
